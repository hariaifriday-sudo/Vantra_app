Executive Summary

What we built, in one sentence.

A working bank simulation where AI drafts, and people decide — end to end, from a customer's first question to a compliance officer's final sign-off.

For customers

Instant answers day or night, identity verification in minutes instead of days, and a direct line to dispute anything the system flags.

For the bank

Agents get AI-drafted recommendations — risk summaries, fraud narratives, SAR drafts — so they review and decide instead of writing from scratch.

For compliance

Every AI output is logged next to the human decision that followed it. Nothing is approved, rejected, or filed by AI alone.

Trust & Governance

Our operating principles.

Published in-product at /trust — visible to every customer, not just this room.

①

A human makes every decision that affects an account, a payment, or an application outcome — approvals, rejections, and fraud confirmations are never fully automated.

②

Every AI-generated field, summary, or recommendation is labeled and shown next to the data it came from, so it can be verified, not just trusted.

③

Customer data answers customer questions — it is not used to train shared models or sold to third parties.

④

Every AI surface has a path to a real person. You can always ask a human.
